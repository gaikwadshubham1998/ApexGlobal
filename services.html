Standard Commercial Terms

Incoterms 2020 Framework

To ensure complete legal and financial clarity between buyer and seller, our trade quotations clearly state the applicable Incoterms 2020 rules:

Clear Scope: While we coordinate freight with accredited forwarders and customs brokers, statutory import tariffs and destination customs clearance remain the buyer's responsibility under standard maritime terms.
FOB

Free on Board (Origin Port)

Seller handles inland transit, export customs clearance, and loads cargo onto the buyer's designated vessel at the Indian port (e.g. JNPT/Mundra).

CIF

Cost, Insurance & Freight

Seller pays for ocean carriage and marine cargo insurance up to the buyer's destination seaport. Risk transfers once cargo is aboard the vessel.

CFR

Cost & Freight (Destination Port)

Seller arranges and pays ocean freight to the destination port, while the buyer covers marine cargo insurance independently.

EXW

Ex Works (Factory / Warehouse)

Buyer assumes all costs and risks from the seller's production facility or warehouse premises, managing domestic transit independently.
