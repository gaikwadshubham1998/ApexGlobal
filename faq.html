Pricing, Quotations & Payment Terms

You can submit an RFQ through our Online Enquiry Form or directly via our WhatsApp Trade Desk. To receive an accurate proforma quotation, please include:
  1. Exact product name and desired grade/specifications.
  2. Target order volume (e.g. number of 20ft/40ft containers or metric tons).
  3. Preferred Incoterm (FOB Indian port or CIF destination port).
  4. Destination seaport name and country.

We safeguard bilateral trade through standard international banking channels:
  • Irrevocable Letter of Credit (LC at Sight): 100% payable against clean shipping documents, issued by an internationally recognized prime bank.
  • Telegraphic Transfer (TT): Typically 30% advance deposit upon contract signing, with the 70% balance payable against scanned copy of the Original Bill of Lading.
Note: We do not offer unsecured open account credit to first-time buyers.

For agricultural commodities, minerals, and bulk packaging, our standard MOQ is one 20-foot Full Container Load (FCL) (approximately 18 to 25 Metric Tons depending on product density). For precision valves, pipe flanges, and fasteners, trial orders of assorted palletized consignments (3 to 5 Metric Tons or 50–100 units) can be accommodated.

Shipping, Documents & Inspections

A complete documentation set is issued and couriered to the buyer or presenting bank:
  • Signed Commercial Invoice and Packing List.
  • Full set (3/3) of Original "Clean On Board" Ocean Bills of Lading.
  • Certificate of Origin issued by the Chamber of Commerce.
  • Phytosanitary Certificate & Fumigation Certificate (for agro commodities & wooden crates).
  • Mill Test Certificate (MTC) & Third-Party Inspection Certificate (SGS/Intertek/TUV) when requested.
