Step-by-Step Flow

How We Coordinate International Trades

From initial requirement definition to cargo discharge at your destination port, our workflow ensures complete operational transparency.

01

Enquiry & Specs

Buyer submits required specifications, target order volume, preferred Incoterm (FOB/CIF), and destination port.

02

Quotation & Samples

We review supply availability, provide firm commercial pricing, and dispatch physical samples when applicable.

03

Contract & LC

Sales contract execution with mutually agreed payment terms (Letter of Credit, TT, or Escrow terms).

04

Packing & Inspection

Batch packaging, container stuffing, and optional pre-shipment third-party inspections (e.g. SGS / Intertek).

05

BL & Port Delivery

Vessel dispatch, issuance of Bill of Lading, document courier, and seamless discharge at destination port.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from International Buyers

Transparent answers to essential questions regarding payment terms, sample requests, and shipping coordination.

We commonly trade under FOB (Free on Board) Indian origin ports (such as Nhava Sheva / JNPT, Mundra, and Chennai) or CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight) and CFR (Cost & Freight) to the buyer's specified destination seaport. Specific terms are clearly outlined in every proforma invoice.

We structure payments to safeguard both buyer and supplier integrity. Standard acceptable terms include Irrevocable, Confirmed Letters of Credit (LC at Sight) issued by prime international banks, or Telegraphic Transfer (TT) with an advance deposit and balance against copy Bill of Lading. Exact payment structure depends on product category and order value.
