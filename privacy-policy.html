Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Transparency regarding how we handle commercial enquiries and contact information.

Last Updated: October 2026. This policy applies to visitors, commercial buyers, and prospective suppliers interacting with ApexGlobal Impex.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when submitting business enquiries, requesting commercial quotations, or contacting us through WhatsApp and email. This may include your full name, business email address, corporate telephone/WhatsApp number, company name, country of operation, and product specifications of interest.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

We process your submitted data exclusively for legitimate international B2B trading purposes:

  • Formulating accurate commercial proforma invoices and quotations.
  • Coordinating sample dispatches and shipping feasibility studies.
  • Communicating via phone, WhatsApp, or email regarding your specific trade mandate.
  • Fulfilling statutory export documentation and bank verification requirements.

3. WhatsApp Communication Disclaimer

When utilizing our WhatsApp click-to-chat features, your message data is processed through WhatsApp LLC's platform in accordance with WhatsApp's independent Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Opening WhatsApp with a prefilled message does not transmit data automatically; you retain full control to review and dispatch your enquiry by clicking Send.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or commercial contact information to third-party brokers or advertisers. Data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with verified operational partners (such as accredited freight forwarders, ocean carriers, and testing labs like SGS/Intertek) solely to execute agreed physical shipments.

5. Data Retention & Security

We maintain commercial-grade security practices to safeguard electronic records. Information relating to executed international contracts is retained in compliance with applicable Indian commercial, tax, and foreign trade regulations.
