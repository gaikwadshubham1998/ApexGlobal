Terms of Trading & Commercial Scope

Legal and operational principles governing inquiries, quotations, and contract formation.

Commercial Framework: International merchant trading involves formal contracts. Website content serves an informational and request-for-quotation (RFQ) purpose only.

1. Nature of Website Information

The product specifications, indicative minimum order quantities (MOQ), and shipping routes displayed on this website represent standard trade benchmarks. Nothing on this website constitutes a binding unilateral offer to sell. Commercial obligations arise solely upon the bilateral execution of a formal Sales Contract or signed Proforma Invoice.

2. Commercial Quotations & Validity

Due to fluctuations in ocean container freight rates, currency exchange rates, and commodity market prices, all formal price quotations are valid strictly for the timeframe specified in the issued Proforma Invoice (typically 3 to 7 business days).

3. Incoterms ® 2020 Governance

All international commercial transactions coordinated through ApexGlobal Impex are interpreted in accordance with the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) Incoterms ® 2020 rules. The allocation of costs, freight, insurance, and risk is formally fixed in the contract (e.g. FOB, CIF, CFR, or EXW).

4. Quality Standards & Inspection Protocol

Where independent inspection (SGS, Intertek, etc.) is agreed upon in the contract, the inspector's certificate issued at the loading port facility serves as definitive evidence of cargo conformity with contract parameters.

5. Governing Law & Jurisdiction

Except where an alternative arbitration mechanism is mutually specified in an executed commercial contract, commercial arrangements are governed by the substantive laws of India, with courts in Mumbai having competent jurisdiction.
